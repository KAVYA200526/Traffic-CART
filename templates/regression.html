Regression Metrics Explained
MAE

Mean Absolute Error: Average of absolute differences between predicted and actual traffic density values.

RMSE

Root Mean Square Error: Square root of the average squared differences, sensitive to larger errors.

R² Score

Coefficient of Determination: Proportion of variance in traffic density explained by the model (0-1).

Lower is Better

For MAE and RMSE, lower values indicate better performance. For R², higher values (closer to 1) are better.
